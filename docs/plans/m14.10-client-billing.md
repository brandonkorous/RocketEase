# M14.10 — Per-client billing (statements)

**Status:** built and verified on the dev server against a local stand-in for Stripe, 2026-09-06 (see the
end of this file). No new screen: a Statements block inside the Economics section of the agency overview,
plus billing contact fields in the existing Set rate dialog, so no mockup was needed. Stripe itself was not
called: RocketEase's Stripe account has no Connect client id yet (a launch chore, below).

## What it is

An agency sends each client a monthly statement — the same fee, rebilled ad spend and rebilled AI the
Economics row already computes — as a Stripe invoice **from the agency's own Stripe account**. RocketEase
never touches the money; it only writes the invoice where the agency's money already lives.

## The decision the plan asked for (Stripe Connect vs Stripe Invoicing)

Both, in the only combination that keeps RocketEase out of the money: **Stripe Connect (Standard
accounts, OAuth `read_write`) to reach the agency's account, and Stripe Invoicing on that account**
through the `Stripe-Account` header. No application fee, no destination charge, nothing on our balance.
The alternative — the agency pasting a restricted API key — would make RocketEase a keeper of someone
else's Stripe secret; Connect is revocable from either side and is what Stripe documents for platforms.

## What Stripe actually says (verified 2026-09-06)

| Thing | Stripe's words | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Authorize | `GET https://connect.stripe.com/oauth/authorize?response_type=code&client_id&scope=read_write&redirect_uri&state`; the redirect must match the platform settings | Connect OAuth reference |
| Token | `POST https://connect.stripe.com/oauth/token` with the platform secret key, `grant_type=authorization_code`, `code` → `stripe_user_id`, `livemode`, `scope`; "Consuming an authorization code more than once revokes the account connection" | same |
| Calls on the account | `Stripe-Account: acct_…` header with the platform's own secret key; "data you create for a Standard account (charges, customers, invoices…) will be visible on their Stripe account" | Connect authentication |
| Revoke | `POST https://connect.stripe.com/oauth/deauthorize` with `client_id` and `stripe_user_id` (Standard accounts only) | Connect OAuth reference |
| Invoice | customer → `invoices.create { collection_method: send_invoice, days_until_due }` → `invoiceitems.create { customer, invoice, amount, currency, description }` → `POST /v1/invoices/{id}/send`: "Stripe finalizes the invoice as soon as you send it" and emails it; statuses draft, open, paid, void, uncollectible | Invoicing integration |
| Paid | listen to `invoice.paid` (covers card payments and out-of-band); events from connected accounts arrive on a separate webhook endpoint stamped with `account` | Invoicing integration; Connect webhooks |

## Decisions

- **One statement per client per month, from the same numbers.** `buildStatement` (pure,
  `lib/agency/statement.ts`) turns the Economics row's inputs into lines: the fee (retainer, or per-post ×
  posts published), then ad spend and AI rebilled at their markups where the rate says so. It reads the
  same `marginInputs` the table reads, so the two can never disagree. An unknown input blocks the whole
  statement with its reason (no rate, hourly billing, no ad account, another currency); a zero total is
  refused as "nothing to bill".
- **The agency's account, not ours.** `agency_billing_account` holds the `acct_…` id per organization
  (migration 0033); every Stripe call carries `stripeAccount`. Disconnecting deauthorizes at Stripe and
  keeps the sent statements as records.
- **The client's billing contact lives on the rate.** `client_rate` gains `billing_name`,
  `billing_email` and the `stripe_customer_id` Stripe made on the agency's account; a statement cannot be
  sent without an email, and the customer is made once.
- **Status follows the invoice.** `client_statement` mirrors Stripe: `sent` (Stripe's `open`), `paid`,
  `void`, `uncollectible`. The Connect webhook updates it only when the event's account is the
  organization's own; "Refresh" re-reads the invoice when a webhook was missed. A send Stripe refused
  leaves a `void` row with the reason — nothing looks sent that was not.
- **Owners and admins only**, like the rest of Economics.

## Definitions the product shows

- **Statement** — this month's fee, rebilled ad spend and rebilled AI for one client, as an invoice from
  your Stripe account. Due 30 days after sending.
- **Sent / Paid / Void** — Stripe's own invoice states, read back from Stripe.

## Launch chore (yours)

In the Stripe dashboard: enable Connect on RocketEase's account, add the OAuth redirect
`https://app.rocketease.com/api/agency/stripe/callback`, copy the client id to Key Vault as
`STRIPE_CONNECT_CLIENT_ID`, and add a webhook endpoint for **events on connected accounts** pointing at
`/api/webhooks/stripe-connect` with `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `invoice.voided`,
`invoice.marked_uncollectible`, `invoice.finalized`; its signing secret is `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.

## Left out on purpose

- Taxes, discounts and credit notes: the agency edits those in its own Stripe dashboard on the draft or
  the sent invoice; the statement here is the lines RocketEase can stand behind.
- Automatic monthly sending: a person presses Send after seeing the lines; a scheduled send is a later
  step once agencies have used the manual one.
- Hourly clients: RocketEase does not track hours, so their statement stays blocked with that reason.

## Live check (2026-09-06)

`apps/platform/e2e/.state/statements-live/` (git-ignored): `stub.ts` stands in for Stripe's API on :5098
(customers, invoices, items, send → `open`, void, retrieve; it refuses any call without
`Stripe-Account: acct_stub_agency`); the dev server ran with `STRIPE_API_HOST`/`PORT` pointing at it and
stand-in keys. The Connect OAuth round trip needs connect.stripe.com and was seeded; the exchange is thin
and typed.

- A fresh agency org with one client, three posts published this month: the Statements block reads
  "✓ Stripe connected · acct_stub_agency · test mode" and the client row "No rate is set for this client."
- Set rate: per post $150 with a billing contact → the row reads "Fee · $450.00 · ○ Not sent"; Preview
  lists "Published posts — September 2026 (3 × $150.00)" and the total.
- Send: the stand-in received a customer, an invoice on the agency's account, one item of 45,000 cents
  and the send; the row reads "→ Sent · to billing@client-a.example" with Open invoice, Refresh and Void;
  the statement stores the invoice id, number and hosted link, and the rate keeps the customer id.
  Preview is gone: a second statement for the month is refused.
- A signed `invoice.paid` for the agency's account: the webhook applied it and the row reads "✓ Paid".
- A signed `invoice.voided` stamped with someone else's account: 200, `applied: false`, "the event's
  account is not this organization's"; the status stayed paid.
- Disconnect: the block offers "Connect your Stripe account" again; the sent statement stays.
