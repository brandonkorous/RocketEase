## What it is

The in-app list of everything that needs a person in a workspace, with filters, day groups, deep
links, and a per-member choice of what reaches the app and what is also emailed. It finishes plan
row 2.9 (the table, the writes and the failure e-mail already existed; the screen was a bare list
and preferences were email-only).

## Decisions

- **Kinds are a closed list** (`lib/notifications/catalog.ts`). `notify()` takes `NotificationKind`,
  so an emitter cannot invent a kind the center cannot label, group or explain. Every kind has an
  icon, a status chip (icon + label, never colour alone), an action label, and the tabs it appears in.
- **A preference covers one or more kinds.** Members choose per preference, per channel: In-app and
  Email. Publishing failures are locked to in-app ("Always"), because a quiet failure is the worst
  outcome this product can have. Stored shape is `{ [pref]: { inApp, email } }`; the pre-M14.2 shape
  (a boolean keyed by kind, meaning email) is still read.
- **Needs action has a definition**: kinds that stay wrong until someone acts — a failed publish or
  reply, a review request, a broken connection, expiring rights. Every tab's count carries its
  definition as a tooltip.
- **Pages, not "load more"** (user decision). 20 per page, real links, so back and shared URLs work.
- **Connection health is now a notification** (`connection.health`). It fires on the TRANSITION into
  `action_required` or `revoked` only — from the channel sync worker and from a failed token refresh
  — to owners, admins and managers, with an email by default. Staying broken is not news twice.
- **Mobile**: Notifications sits at the top of the More drawer (pages.md lists it as a mobile surface).
- **Left out on purpose**: "Weekly summary" and "Performance alerts" from the old settings mockup
  (nothing defines them), and a Security row (no in-app emitter exists; new sign-ins are emailed by
  the auth hooks). The footer says digests and quiet hours are not offered yet.
