# M14.3 — Approval due dates

**Status:** built and verified live against the dev server, 2026-09-05. No new screen, so no new
mockup: the work extends Approvals (`images/approvals.png`, which already shows "Due today, 10:30 AM"
in red under the assignee), Home, the post page, the composer and the agency overview.

## What it is

Finishes plan row 3.4. `approval_request.due_at` existed before this but nothing could set it (the
policy window always won), nothing reminded anyone, and "overdue" was red text in one list. Now the
requester picks a deadline, one definition of overdue drives every surface, the reviewers get one
reminder, and a pending deadline can be moved.

## Definitions

- **Due**: the requester's time when given (composer "Review due", API `dueAt`), else the policy
  window (`approval_policy.due_hours`, 24 h without a policy). A time less than a minute ahead of now
  is refused: "The due time must be in the future." (`dueAtFor`, `lib/approvals/rules.ts`.)
- **Overdue**: `state = pending` and `due_at < now`. A decided or withdrawn request is never overdue
  (`isOverdue`). Every surface calls this one function.
- **Concerns you**: an overdue request you can decide (`canDecide`) or you asked for (`concernsMe`).
  The sidebar badge, Home and the agency overview count exactly this — navigation.md allows a badge
  only for an actionable count, and "overdue approvals" is its first example.
- **Reminder**: one per deadline. The `approval.remind` job runs every 5 minutes (pg-boss cron,
  `worker/ticks.ts`) and CLAIMS each newly overdue request by stamping `reminded_at` before anything is
  sent, so two workers or a retry cannot remind twice. Recipients: the assignee when there is one,
  else every member whose role is in the request's approver roles, minus the requester under
  separation of duty (`reminderRecipients`). Kind `approval.overdue` — chip "Overdue" (alert icon,
  error tone), button "Review", in the Needs action and Approvals tabs — under the **Approval
  requests** preference, in-app and email by default. Moving the deadline forward clears
  `reminded_at`, so the new deadline gets its own reminder.
- **Change due time**: reviewers who can decide the request and the requester, on a pending request
  only. Audited as `approval.due_changed` with before/after.

## Left out on purpose

- A "due soon" warning. Policies express the window in hours and nothing in the docs says how soon
  "soon" is, so there is no number to defend. One reminder at the deadline is what onboarding.md
  reserves email for ("approval requests/due reminders").
- A separate reminder to the requester. They see the state on Home, in the sidebar count and on the
  post banner; a reminder to someone who cannot act is noise.
- The mockup's "Due date: Any time" filter. The Overdue tab is the one question it would answer.
