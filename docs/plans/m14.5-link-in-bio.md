# M14.5 — Link-in-bio page

**Status:** built and verified live against the dev server, 2026-09-05. Mockups approved the same day
(`images/link-in-bio.png`, `images/link-in-bio-public.png`, `images/link-in-bio-empty.png`); editable
canvas: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/705bb01b-3a0d-46e5-a585-0f893ac96f5c. Decision recorded in
`docs/originals/navigation.md` (route map) and `pages.md` (Brand): the page is a **Brand tool**, not a
sidebar item, because it is built from the kit.

## What it is

One public page per workspace at `/l/:slug` — avatar, name, bio, ordered links, an optional strip of
the latest published posts, and buttons in black or the brand primary — edited under **Brand › Link in
bio** with a phone preview beside the form. Hidden until switched Live.

## Definitions

- **Address**: `/l/<slug>`, where the slug is the workspace's own slug, or the first free variant of it
  (`piggles`, `piggles-2`, …). Workspace slugs are unique per organisation only, so the page's slug is
  unique on its own. It is set once, at creation; there is no rename (`lib/bio/rules.ts#nextFreeSlug`).
- **Live / Hidden**: a hidden or unknown address is ONE 404 — a dead link never confirms a page exists.
  Hidden keeps the address, the links and the counts.
- **A link the page shows**: switched on, with a title and an address (`isPublicLink`). "Add link"
  makes an empty row to fill in; the public page skips it until both are there. Addresses are
  normalised: a bare host gets `https://`; anything not `http(s)` is refused (`normalizeUrl`).
  20 links at most.
- **A click**: one row in `bio_link_click`, written by the redirect route `/l/:slug/go/:linkId` before
  the 302. One click, one row; nothing is deduplicated or estimated. "Last 7 days" is the rolling last
  7 × 24 hours; "all time" is every row. A link nobody opened reads 0 because there are 0 rows.
  Removing a link removes its rows (the confirm says so); switching it off keeps them.
- **Latest posts**: the newest published variants of ONE connected channel that have a picture, up to
  6, each linking to the live post (`lib/bio/posts.ts`). Nothing is fetched from the network for this.
- **Avatar**: the kit's favicon, else mark, else primary logo file, presigned per render; "Initial" is
  the fallback and the alternative.
- **Buttons**: black by rule, or the kit's primary swatch when one exists; the text colour flips to
  black on a light brand colour (`buttonColors`, WCAG luminance). Text elsewhere stays black on white.
- **Who may edit**: `workspace.settings` — the same bar as the kit. Every member sees the editor and
  the counts. Every write is audited as `workspace.bio_page`.

## Left out on purpose

- Custom domains (the plan row says "later"); the address is on the RocketEase host.
- Writing clicks into `conversion_fact`: the tracking layer keys everything to a `tracking_source`
  and to UTM medium, and a bio click is neither a site conversion nor an ad conversion. The click log
  is adjacent (same day-in-workspace-zone shape) and can feed Analytics as its own row later.
- Renaming the address, and per-visitor or per-referrer breakdowns (nothing is recorded per visitor).
