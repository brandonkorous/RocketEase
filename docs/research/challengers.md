## (a) Self-hosted movement
Motivations: cost escape (Mixpost $299 + $5–20/mo VPS breaks even vs $29–99/mo SaaS in 3–10 months), data ownership, unlimited accounts without seat/profile taxes, extensibility (Postiz AGPL). Trade-offs: Docker skill, maintenance, crippled free tier. Both monetize via perpetual license or cheap cloud, not per-seat.

## (b) What agencies want in 2026
- Client separation without workarounds past ~20 clients; per-workspace pricing is the tax.
- Approvals that don't live in Slack — every revision cycle is unpaid time; link-based approval with no client sign-up is the pattern named.
- Automated multi-client reporting.
- White-label with a real client-facing surface: logo, domain, email identity, dashboard.
- Economics: tools eat 8–15% of gross revenue; 5–10 hrs/week lost to tool friction; white-label wholesale $300–3,000/client/mo at 40–60% markup.

## (c) AI: used vs ignored
Sociality.io 2026 / Social Media Examiner 2026 (681 marketers): 89.7% use AI weekly, 64.1% daily. Used: ideation/trend research 59.5%, analytics & reporting 59.5%, captions 45.9%, visuals/video 40.5%. Ignored: autonomous automation/optimization ~16%. **~80% edit AI output moderately or extensively.** 50% of Gen Z have unfollowed accounts judged AI-generated. Credible shift is API/MCP-first distribution (Blotato, Postiz, Metricool Advanced): the agent writes, the tool publishes.

## Underserved niches / whitespace
1. Mid-market between Metricool ($53) and Sprinklr ($50k): $150–500 flat, multi-workspace, unlimited seats.
2. Approval UX + analytics in one product at agency scale.
3. Per-client billing / reselling built in — only Vendasta-class platforms, not social-native.
4. Unlimited-seat pricing (Sendible's genuine differentiator).
5. Publishing reliability as a feature — reconciliation, retry transparency, per-channel health.
6. Self-hostable / license-owned agency tier with approvals + white-label.
7. Agent/MCP-first API surface.
8. Honest AI positioning: assisted drafting + brand-voice grounding + human gate.
