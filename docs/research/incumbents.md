## Recurring customer pain points (ranked by frequency)

1. **Per-seat pricing that punishes team growth.** #1 Sprout complaint; Agorapulse 5-person Professional team = $745/mo; Hootsuite per seat + 10-account cap. Structurally hostile to agencies.
2. **Renewal price shocks, billing/cancellation friction.** Hootsuite renewals "almost doubled"; BBB C+; Later Trustpilot ~1.3/5 (>80% one-star, billing) vs 4.5 on G2; Sprinklr self-serve sunset forces SMBs into enterprise sales.
3. **Best features paywalled/add-ons.** Sprout sentiment requires Advanced; Listening is not in any tier; "surprise-upgrade" experience.
4. **Analytics shallow, rigid, short-retention.** Hootsuite aggregate-only; Agorapulse retains 1 month–2 years; Emplifi coarse granularity; Buffer thin.
5. **Complexity and slow/clunky UI at enterprise tier.** Sprinklr top G2 cons: Complex Usage, Learning Curve; Khoros "UI from the '90s", slow calendar; Sprout inbox filters buried, 3-week ramp.
6. **Publishing failures — token expiry and silent/ambiguous errors.** Meta ~60-day token expiry; Adobe Express Feb 2025 outage; "every post says failed but it hasn't"; Emplifi duplicate posts persisting after tickets.
7. **Instagram/TikTok capability ceilings blamed on tools.** No `scheduled_publish_time` for Stories; no interactive stickers via API; licensed-music Reels can't auto-publish; TikTok carousels only recently. Users experience platform limits as product defects.
8. **Support responsiveness.** Sprinklr slow; Hootsuite "slow to reach a human"; Later systemic.
9. **Agency gaps:** white-label, client dashboards, granular permissions — only Sendible, Agorapulse, Sprout built for it.
10. **Approvals gated behind higher tiers** (Buffer Team-only).

## What none of them do well

1. Honest, tier-independent conversion attribution (add-on, GA proxy, or absent; agencies advised to run separate incrementality tests).
2. Truthful publishing state — "failed but posted"/duplicates show retry-on-ambiguous; nobody markets reconciliation or idempotency.
3. Setting expectations about platform limits — no per-channel capability contract.
4. Pricing that fits agencies — per-seat and per-channel both tax many clients / many low-intensity collaborators; reviewers billed like operators.
5. Analytics durability — short retention, aggregate exports.
6. Never showing a missing metric as zero.
7. Enterprise depth without enterprise onboarding cost — the middle is where complaints cluster.
