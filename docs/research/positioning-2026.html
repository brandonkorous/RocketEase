RocketEase · competitive positioning · 28 Aug 2026

The Honest Social OS

Every incumbent charges per seat, retries ambiguous publishes into duplicates, hides platform limits until they fail, and renders a missing metric as zero. We already built the opposite of all four. The gap to close is making that visible — and adding the handful of surfaces buyers expect before they'll look.

Position: the social platform that never lies to you — about what posted, what it cost, what a number means, or what a network can't do — priced so your clients and reviewers are free.

02

What we already own

Verified in code. None of these are marketed by any competitor because none of them do them. They are the story — today they're invisible because they live in worker code and schema.

Publish truth

Reconcile before retry

publish.execute is retryLimit: 0; the worker calls findPublication on ambiguous errors. Idempotency keys per variant. Nightly reconcile.

Capability truth

Per-channel limits with reasons

Disabled controls explain themselves ("Pinterest: no comments API"). Versioned validation derived from what the account actually granted.

Metric truth

Contracts, not numbers

24 metrics with formula, provider mapping, freshness, caveats and DEFINITIONS_VERSION stamped into every export and shared report.

Attribution truth

No double-counting, no fake zeros

Paid utm_medium → ad platform; everything else → site source. Every unavailable metric carries its reason.

Spend safety

Step-up before money moves

Re-auth, budget caps, currency checks, explicit confirmation and audit before any boost. Automations can't pause ads or answer reviews without a person.

Agency surface

White-label reports that expire

Branded documents, signed revocable /r/:token links with passcode, rate limit, audited views; double-opt-in external recipients; SSO + SCIM.

The moves

Ordered by leverage ÷ effort. "Market" means the code exists and needs a surface, copy, and a landing-page claim. Sizes are rough build estimates on the current codebase.

  1. Flat per-workspace pricing, unlimited seats, free client reviewersAttack the #1 complaint and the agency margin leak (15–30% lost to approval friction) in one packaging decision. Client-approver role already exists with object-scoped links. Undercuts Planable's per-workspace add-on stacking and Sprout's per-seat.
    DecisionM8 billing
  2. "Publish receipts" — surface reconciliation as a featureEvery post shows a timeline: validated → sent → confirmed-by-provider (with remote id) or reconciled-after-timeout. Landing claim: "Never a duplicate, never a phantom failure." Data is already in post_variant + audit; needs UI + copy.
    Market~1 wk
  3. Capability contract as a public page + in-app "why not"Ship a public, per-network "what we can and can't do, and why" page generated from packages/providers capability data. Turns the incumbents' silent failures into our transparency story.
    Market~3 days
  4. Metric-continuity layer for Meta's reach → "viewers" swapMeta retires Post/Page Reach and Video/Story Impressions in 2026. Dual-track old/new, annotate the break on every chart, keep history honest. The registry (DEFINITIONS_VERSION, revisions in metric_fact) is built for exactly this; nobody else is set up to handle it cleanly.
    Build~1 wk
  5. Rights & authorisation clocksExtend rightsExpiresAt to Spark Ad codes (7/30/60/365 d), Partnership Ads grants, and paid-usage licences (30/60/90 d). Block or warn when a boost would run past expiry. Small schema + composer/promote check.
    Build~1 wk
  6. Per-item publish cost & quota previewX: $0.015/post, $0.20 for posts with links; YouTube: 1,600 units/Short ≈ 6/day. Show cost/quota impact in the composer before queuing, with a daily quota gauge on Connected accounts. No tool surfaces this.
    Build~1 wk
  7. AI disclosure per destinationOne "contains synthetic media" flag in the composer → YouTube AI attribute, TikTok label, Meta path; recorded in audit. EU AI Act Art. 50 machine-readable marking applies from 2 Aug 2026. Compliance angle that fits our audit-first architecture.
    Build~1 wk
  8. Public API + MCP serverThe drafting layer is moving to agents (Blotato, Postiz, Metricool Advanced). Expose create-draft / submit-for-approval / schedule / report as an API and MCP tools — with approval gates intact. Positions us as the trusted publishing layer under any AI. Server actions already exist; needs API keys + routes.
    Build~2 wk
  9. AI that drafts, never publishesCaption variants per network, repurpose long → short, reply drafts in the inbox grounded in brand voice from workspace.settings — always landing as drafts in the existing approval flow. Adoption data says this is what actually gets used (ideation 60%, captions 46%); autonomy (~16%) is not. Marketing line: "AI drafts. A person presses send."
    Gap~2–3 wk
  10. Evergreen recycling + hashtag sets + CSV importTable stakes on every challenger (SocialBee, Publer). Cheap to add on the existing template/lineage and bulk-reschedule code. Not a differentiator; their absence is a disqualifier in comparisons.
    Gap~2 wk
  11. Review management via Google Business ProfileMeta declares reviews: true but no adapter fetches them. Vista Social bundles GBP/Yelp/Trustpilot reviews and wins SMB with it. Fix the over-declaration; add a GBP adapter. Fits the inbox's existing review kind and the human-only reply gate.
    Gap~2 wk
  12. Per-client cost attribution & margin report (agency)Agencies mark up 40–60% and bill by hand. A per-workspace usage/spend/time roll-up on the agency overview, exportable to invoicing, is unclaimed by any social-native tool. Depends on billing model.
    Laterpost-M8

Don't chase: broad social listening (bifurcated $29 vs $20k market, no coherent middle and heavy data cost), employee advocacy, link-in-bio, a native mobile app before PWA. All are checkbox features where incumbents are entrenched and none reinforces the honesty positioning.

04

Positioning copy

Spec-compliant: no invented proof, no benchmarks. Claims below are about our behaviour, which we can guarantee.

  • Never a duplicate, never a phantom failure. We confirm with the network before we ever retry.
  • Every number has a definition. Formula, source, freshness and caveats travel with every report and export.
  • Missing is never zero. When a metric can't be reported, you see why.
  • We tell you what a network can't do — before you hit publish.
  • Nobody pays to say yes. Unlimited seats. Clients and reviewers are always free.
  • AI drafts. A person presses send. Nothing publishes, spends, or answers a customer without a human.
05

Caveats

Everything above the pricing decision is unproven live. All six real provider adapters (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, X), GA4 and Shopify are code-complete but have only ever run against the in-repo mock. The reliability story is only credible once credentials exist and a beta publishes real posts. That remains the blocking item.

One over-declaration to fix now: Meta capabilities say reviews: true but nothing ingests reviews. Our whole pitch is honesty; the capability table must be exactly right.
